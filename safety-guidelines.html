Safety Guidelines

Last updated: 20 November 2026
A good leisure experience starts with clear information and responsible behaviour. These guidelines apply to customers, guests and venue owners using LeisureLoop.

1. Before you request a space

  • Read the listing, age restrictions, capacity, facilities, accessibility information and venue rules.
  • Ask the owner about supervision, lifeguards, changing facilities, first aid, parking and any activity-specific risks.
  • Confirm the exact address, date, time, total price and what is included before travelling.
  • Do not assume a pool, sports facility or other activity is supervised unless the venue explicitly confirms it.

2. During your visit

  • Follow all instructions from venue staff and posted safety signs.
  • Do not exceed the stated group size or use facilities for activities they are not designed for.
  • For swimming, children must be actively supervised by a responsible adult. Never rely solely on flotation devices or assume a lifeguard is present.
  • Avoid participation when impaired by alcohol or drugs, and do not bring prohibited or dangerous items.
  • Respect the property, staff, neighbours and other guests.

3. Venue owner responsibilities

  • List only spaces you own or are authorised to operate.
  • Keep facilities reasonably safe, clean and maintained; comply with applicable licences, permissions and safety requirements.
  • Disclose material hazards, restrictions, accessibility limitations, capacity limits and supervision arrangements accurately.
  • Provide appropriate staff, emergency procedures and safety equipment for the activities offered.
  • Do not publish misleading photos, amenities or availability.

4. Emergencies and incidents

In an emergency, contact local emergency services and venue staff immediately. Do not wait for a response from LeisureLoop. Report serious safety incidents, suspected fraud or dangerous listings to the platform using its official support contact once published.

5. Platform role

LeisureLoop is a discovery and booking-request platform. It does not operate venues, provide lifeguards, supervise activities or replace the owner’s safety duties or a visitor’s own judgement. A listing on the platform is not a safety certification or inspection.

Important: These guidelines do not replace venue-specific safety instructions, professional supervision or applicable laws and standards.

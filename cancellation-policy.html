Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026
LeisureLoop currently helps users send booking requests to venue owners. Online payments are not currently collected by LeisureLoop. This page covers cancellation and rescheduling; see the separate Refund Policy for refund handling.

1. Request stage

A booking request is not a confirmed reservation until the venue owner or authorised operator accepts it and communicates the final details. You may withdraw a pending request by contacting the venue or using any available request controls on the platform.

2. After a venue confirms

Before accepting, ask the venue to clearly explain its cancellation, rescheduling, late-arrival, no-show and refund rules. Venue-specific terms may apply, but they must be communicated before you agree to the booking and must comply with applicable law.

3. Cancellation by a customer

Contact the venue as early as possible using the contact details provided for the booking. The venue’s disclosed policy will determine whether a refund, credit or reschedule is available for any amount paid directly to it, subject to applicable law.

4. Cancellation by a venue

If a venue cannot honour an accepted booking, it should notify the customer promptly and explain available alternatives. Any refund of an amount paid directly to the venue must be arranged between the venue and customer in accordance with the agreed terms and applicable law.

5. Payments and refunds through LeisureLoop

LeisureLoop does not currently collect booking payments or process booking refunds through the website. If you paid a venue directly, contact that venue for a refund or payment dispute. Never share passwords, OTPs, full card details or UPI PINs with anyone claiming to arrange a refund.

6. Disputes and support

Keep booking confirmations, payment receipts and relevant messages. If you cannot resolve a concern with the venue, contact LeisureLoop through the official support contact published on the website. We may help pass on information or review a platform-related complaint, but we do not guarantee recovery of money paid directly to a venue.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated if LeisureLoop introduces platform payments or new booking features. The version shown on this page applies subject to mandatory consumer rights and applicable law.

Before launch: Have an Indian lawyer review this policy and ensure the venue onboarding process requires owners to disclose cancellation rules before a customer confirms.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026
This policy explains how LeisureLoop handles personal information when you visit our website, create an account, submit a venue, or send a booking request. Please read it before using the platform.

1. Who we are

“LeisureLoop”, “we”, “us” or “our” refers to the operator of the LeisureLoop platform. Before public launch, insert the full legal name of the operating individual or entity and its registered/business address here. LeisureLoop currently operates as an online discovery and booking-request marketplace for leisure spaces.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account details: name, email address, account role and authentication-related information.
  • Venue details: owner or authorised contact details, venue name, address or approximate location, photos, facilities, capacity, prices, availability and rules submitted for a listing.
  • Booking-request details: requested venue, date, time, group size, messages and status of a request.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting us or reporting a concern.
  • Technical information: device/browser details, IP address, diagnostic logs and security events that may be generated when you use the website.
  • Location: approximate or device location only if you choose a location-based feature and grant permission through your device or browser.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate the marketplace, display venue listings, send and manage booking requests, communicate service updates, review owner submissions, prevent fraud and misuse, troubleshoot the website, improve our services, and meet legal obligations.

4. How information is shared

We share only information reasonably needed to operate the service. For example, relevant booking-request details may be shared with the venue owner so they can respond. We may use service providers for website hosting, database/authentication, email delivery, security and technical operations. Those providers process information under their own terms and applicable contractual safeguards. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect users, the platform or others.

We do not sell personal information as a business model. We do not publish private account credentials or passwords.

5. Payments

At the time of this policy, LeisureLoop is operating as a booking-request platform and does not collect online booking payments through the website. If payment processing is introduced, this policy and the applicable payment terms will be updated before that feature is made available. Do not send card, bank or UPI credentials through free-text messages.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The website and its authentication or security providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember necessary preferences, protect accounts and keep the service working. Browser settings can be used to control or clear stored data; disabling essential storage may prevent sign-in or other features from working.

7. Retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including account operation, dispute handling, security, recordkeeping and legal compliance. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a grievance. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it. Account deletion may not immediately remove records that must be retained for security, legal or dispute purposes.

9. Children

LeisureLoop is not intended for children to create accounts or independently arrange bookings. A parent, guardian or responsible adult should make arrangements involving minors and supervise their participation at a venue.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform changes. The revised date will appear at the top of this page. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use, subject to applicable law.

11. Contact and grievances

For privacy requests, questions or complaints, contact the LeisureLoop operator using the official support email and postal address published on the website. Before launch, publish a monitored support email address, the operator’s legal name and a valid correspondence address here.

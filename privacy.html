Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026
This policy explains how LeisureLoop handles personal information when you visit our website, create an account, submit a venue, or send a booking request. Please read it before using the platform.

1. Who we are

“LeisureLoop”, “we”, “us” or “our” refers to the operator of the LeisureLoop platform. Business disclosure still required: “LeisureLoop” is the trading/brand name. The legal name of the person or entity operating the service, its principal business address and its privacy/grievance contact have not yet been provided and must be published before public launch. LeisureLoop currently operates as an online discovery and booking-request marketplace for leisure spaces.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account details: name, email address, account role and authentication-related information.
  • Venue details: owner or authorised contact details, venue name, address or approximate location, photos, facilities, capacity, prices, availability and rules submitted for a listing.
  • Booking-request details: requested venue, date, time, group size, messages and status of a request.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting us or reporting a concern.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser/device details, request metadata and security/diagnostic logs that may be received by the hosting, authentication or third-party resource providers.
  • Location: location is optional. If you press “Use my current location” and grant browser permission, the browser sends coordinates to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to identify a city. The site stores the resulting city preference in local browser storage; it is not necessary to provide location to browse.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, operate the marketplace, display venue listings, send and manage booking requests, communicate service updates, review owner submissions, prevent fraud and misuse, troubleshoot the website, improve our services, and meet legal obligations.

4. How information is shared

We share only information reasonably needed for the stated purpose. Booking details are shared with the selected venue owner so they can respond; venue details are processed to review and display listings. The current code connects to Vercel for hosting, Supabase for authentication/database services, public CDNs (including jsDelivr and unpkg) to load the Supabase client, Google Fonts for typography, Unsplash for the homepage image, and OpenStreetMap Nominatim for reverse geocoding only when you actively request current location. A provider may receive technical request data such as your IP address and browser metadata. We have not verified the final contractual data-processing terms, hosting regions or international-transfer arrangements for every provider; these must be checked before launch. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect users, the platform or others.

We do not sell personal information as a business model. We do not publish private account credentials or passwords.

5. Payments

At the time of this policy, LeisureLoop is operating as a booking-request platform and does not collect online booking payments through the website. If payment processing is introduced, this policy and the applicable payment terms will be updated before that feature is made available. Do not send card, bank or UPI credentials through free-text messages.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The current site uses browser local storage for authentication session persistence, a chosen city, a role-preview setting and dismissal of the storage notice. The code review did not find configured analytics or advertising trackers. See our Cookie PolicyRefund Policy for the current storage keys, external resources and limitations of this review.

7. Retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including account operation, dispute handling, security, recordkeeping and legal compliance. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data minimisation, consent and your rights

We aim to collect only information needed for account security, booking requests, venue review and service operation. Location is optional; do not provide sensitive personal information in listing descriptions or free-text fields. Where consent is the legal basis, it should be specific, informed and given through a clear affirmative action. You may withdraw consent using a method that is reasonably easy to access; withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully completed and may mean we cannot provide the related feature.

Subject to the commencement and application of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, individuals may have rights to access information about processing, seek correction or erasure, withdraw consent and raise a grievance. Requests should be sent to the published grievance contact. Some records may need to be retained where lawfully required for security, legal compliance or dispute handling. Account deletion may not immediately erase records that must lawfully be retained.

9. Children

LeisureLoop is not intended for children to create accounts or independently arrange bookings. A parent, guardian or responsible adult should make arrangements involving minors and supervise their participation at a venue.

10. Personal data breaches

We will maintain an incident-response process and, when required by applicable law and the provisions in force, notify affected individuals and the relevant authority about a personal data breach with the prescribed information and timelines. The process must include containment, investigation, recordkeeping and steps to reduce harm. This is a policy commitment; the operational response plan and responsible contact must be established before launch.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform changes. The revised date will appear at the top of this page. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use, subject to applicable law.

12. Contact and grievances

For privacy requests and grievances, the operating entity must publish a monitored email address and a valid correspondence address. Business details required before launch: (1) full legal name of the proprietor/company/LLP operating LeisureLoop; (2) principal business or registered address; (3) monitored privacy/grievance email; and (4) grievance contact person and telephone number, if applicable. These have not yet been provided, so this policy is not ready to serve as the final statutory notice.
